Curated real 3D asset library

A smaller library of 22 actual objects and object arrangements relevant to 3DGS, rather than 50 largely unsuitable glTF testing samples. No cloth sheets, material grids, morph-test primitives, fox or skull. Models load over the internet and retain source PBR textures. Their source licences are CC0 or CC BY 4.0 (some mixed); retain the original attribution. Browser/WebGL load success must be checked on your device.

Select a model and press Load selected model. A model is only considered ready after its GLB geometry and textures load successfully. Source and licence information will appear here.

Render image dataset

ZIP includes train/images, test/images, optional masks, camera-to-world and COLMAP poses, capture manifest, and reconstruction instructions. Large high-resolution datasets may exhaust browser memory; start with 96 views at 1280 × 720 to test your computer. All built-in capture resolutions are landscape 16:9. Curated assets are restricted to CC0 / CC BY 4.0 style licences suitable for research use; keep the bundled attribution text with any JPEG or paper figures.

Repeatability and import

The loaded asset is stationary; lighting and materials remain fixed. The held-out test views are excluded from training, COLMAP feature extraction/matching/triangulation, and MVS. Keep attribution with any JPEG-RF datasets, publications, figures or presentations.
