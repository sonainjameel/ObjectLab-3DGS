3D cameras · drag, orbit & fine-tune

Move cameras directly in 3D: click and drag a camera box. Orbit mode moves around the subject without losing the look-at target; Free mode slides a camera through the viewer plane; Height mode lifts or lowers it. You can also fine-tune with live orbit sliders and exact coordinates. Camera boxes are editor-only and do not appear in exported images.

Generate positions or select a camera box.

Tip: Orbit drag = left/right azimuth, up/down elevation. Free drag = translate in screen plane, still pointing at the look-at target. Height drag = move up/down only. Drag empty space to rotate the editor view. The selected camera stays visible even with many camera views.

Render image dataset

ZIP includes train/images, test/images, optional masks, camera-to-world and COLMAP poses, capture manifest, and reconstruction instructions. Large high-resolution datasets may exhaust browser memory; start with 96 views at 1280 × 720 to test your computer. All three capture resolutions are landscape 16:9. Curated assets are restricted to CC0 / CC BY 4.0 style licences suitable for research use; keep the bundled attribution text with any JPEG or paper figures.

Repeatability and import

The loaded asset is stationary; lighting and materials remain fixed. The held-out test views are excluded from training, COLMAP feature extraction/matching/triangulation, and MVS. Keep attribution with any JPEG-RF datasets, publications, figures or presentations.
